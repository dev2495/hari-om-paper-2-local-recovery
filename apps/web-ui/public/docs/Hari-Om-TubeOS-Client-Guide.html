Change comparison

What changed and why

Workspace navigation

Previous issue

Hover-dependent navigation and crowded module tabs.

Candidate behavior

An explicit compact or expanded rail, grouped workspaces, a keyboard command menu, and a mobile navigation dialog. Planning has dedicated overview, winder, oven, process and slitting pages; reconciliation has workspace, actuals, drift and history pages.

Appearance and comfort

Previous issue

Mixed panel styles, oversized headers and hard-coded light colors.

Candidate behavior

Pearl surfaces, ink text and teal actions; semantic status colors; Light, Dark and System appearance; compact table density; bounded motion and reduced-motion support.

Page structure

Previous issue

Large decorative headers competed with operational information.

Candidate behavior

Compact page headers, clear actions, consistent summaries, filters, registers and detail panels. Existing business actions and permissions are retained.

Customer PO scheduling

Previous issue

Pending demand and scheduling needed a unified whole-order flow.

Candidate behavior

Review pending customer orders, required quantities and material demand. Preview a whole-PO customer delivery schedule, inspect dated quantities, then save the reviewed version. Schedule production separately on machine and shift capacity slots. Split allocations and stale previews remain explicit.

Monthly procurement calendar

Previous issue

The client workbook workflow was absent from the recovered checkout.

Candidate behavior

A monthly material schedule supports item-specific demand, frozen job BOMs, accepted external finished goods and supplier arrivals. A reviewed workbook preview is required before applying changes.

Supplier promises

Previous issue

Delivery promises and receipts needed a common history.

Candidate behavior

Supplier schedule allocations link promised deliveries, receipt allocation and history. Future confirmed supply is separated from available stock.

Purchase approval

Previous issue

Changes to vendor terms needed durable review evidence.

Candidate behavior

Multi-item purchase orders use numbered documents, revision snapshots and maker-checker approval. Changes after approval require a new reviewed revision.

Purchase register

Previous issue

Browser-side filtering could miss orders beyond a fetched page.

Candidate behavior

Search and paging run on the server. The shareable URL keeps query, status, page and size. Matching-order summaries cover all filtered results and keep units separate.

Material inward

Previous issue

Receipt entry needed both PO-backed and manual flows.

Candidate behavior

Manual and PO-backed GRNs retain material identity, measured reels/coils, invoice evidence and stock batch links. ALL-plant scope is read-only for plant-specific execution.

Commercial differences

Previous issue

Invoice rates and approved PO rates could be confused.

Candidate behavior

Commercial differences, debit-note actions and historical batch costs remain distinct from QC disposition. An invoice disagreement is not a quality release.

Labels and registers

Previous issue

Operators needed durable source identity in outputs.

Candidate behavior

AT/QR labels, receipt details and register exports retain document and lot context. Mixed-unit quantities and rates are displayed with their own units.

Quality controls

Previous issue

Quality criteria and stock disposition needed linked evidence.

Candidate behavior

Available QC flows connect incoming and stage inspection, frozen lot criteria, measurements and hold/disposition. Packing measurements cannot substitute for final QC; dispatch independently requires a current passing final inspection. Approved criteria may be marked Critical: cannot be waived. Their frozen policy blocks administrator concessions and stage overrides after failure. The release appendix identifies remaining advanced quality and race-condition checks.

Dashboard meaning

Previous issue

Some displayed targets and sparklines were not supported by the metric.

Candidate behavior

The owner dashboard removes the assumed 92% OTIF target and quantity sparklines from currency metrics. Loading, unavailable and failed data are visible; cards link to relevant workspaces.

Winder release and planning

Previous issue

Winder capability checks could stop scheduling on an otherwise available machine.

Candidate behavior

Select a winder number or name at release as a hint. Planning and execution show geometry or mandrel advisories without blocking; plant, availability, capacity and QC controls remain. Large quantities split across capacity slots.

BOM geometry and dispatch dates

Previous issue

Material calculations could use sample dimensions and zero inner diameter; dispatch without a date could use the previous UTC day.

Candidate behavior

New BOM calculations use saved specification geometry, preserve fractional lengths and honor saved nominal dimensions. New dispatches freeze the India business date for the challan and inventory posting; retries retain that date. Existing frozen jobs and historical transactions are not rewritten.

Partial dispatch and phone entry

Previous issue

A partial shipment could fall back to the full packed quantity, and sealing could enter a URL redirect loop.

Candidate behavior

Sealing honors the entered shipment quantity, accepts the UI URL directly, and preserves retry identity. The phone form stacks customer, transport and item details; printed challans retain their document layout.

Help and handover

Previous issue

Help did not explain all recovered flows or new controls.

Candidate behavior

A searchable guide library, route-specific instructions and matching downloadable HTML/PDF cover operator steps, field rules, controls and outputs.

01 / Daily start point

Control Room Guide

Workspace: /dashboard

Use this page to read company posture before opening any transaction screen.

Daily management loop

  1. Review alerts

    Start with overdue orders, stock risk, QC holds, and plant blockers.

  2. Pick scope

    Switch role or plant only when the task needs a narrower operating view.

  3. Open work

    Move into sales, planning, inventory, quality, or dispatch from the current exception.

  4. Close loop

    Return here after action to confirm the signal moved.

Field rules

  • Plant switcher controls write scope for plant users.
  • Owner and Admin can read all plants and still use plant filters for execution.
  • Critical alerts should be cleared by completing the source workflow, not by hiding the alert.

Actions

  • Use Jump to workspace or Command/Ctrl + K to find a workflow by name. Open Appearance settings for Light, Dark or System and table density.
  • Open the notification center for recent operational messages.
  • Use the guide button in the header to understand the page you are on.

Control checks

  • Check that pending approvals, QC holds, and dispatch queues are reviewed daily.
  • Verify that books lock status is visible before editing historical stock.

Expected outputs

  • Clean shift handoff
  • Open exception list
  • Confirmed plant scope

Sales Order and Release Guide

Workspace: /sales-orders

Capture customer demand, confirm the specification, approve the order, and release it to planning only when the commercial details are correct.

Order release flow

  1. Create order

    Select customer, product specification, parchment, quantity, and due date.

  2. Approve

    Approver validates demand, customer terms, and any exception notes.

  3. Release

    Select a winder number or name as a release hint. The released job enters the planning queue; the planner may use another available winder.

Field rules

Actions

  • Create new order from Sales Orders.
  • Approve and release selected approved orders.
  • Open order audit to review who changed demand or release status.

Control checks

  • Do not release an order without a valid specification.
  • Review partial release quantity before sending to planning.
  • Use tracking before dispatch if customer asks for status.

Expected outputs

  • Approved demand
  • Planner release
  • Order audit trail

04 / Procurement control

Purchase and Vendor Guide

Workspace: /purchase

Create a numbered PO draft, submit its immutable revision for maker-checker approval, and revise any vendor-agreed change before stores receive it.

Controlled PO flow

  1. Create draft

    Select RM/PM or OT. The server reserves RP-PM/nn or OT/nn; users never type the PO number.

  2. Submit revision

    The complete vendor, lines, rates, specifications and dates become a review snapshot.

  3. Approve

    A different authorized user approves or rejects that exact revision.

  4. Revise changes

    Any later vendor, quantity, rate, specification or delivery change creates a new revision and needs approval again.

Field rules

  • Paper ordered and fulfilled quantity is kg; expected reel or coil count is a separate planning field.
  • An approved rate is immutable evidence and cannot be overwritten during inward.
  • Batch price belongs to inward stock. Planning standard costs never overwrite PO rates, invoice rates or historical batch valuation.
  • Draft edits require a reason and version check after the first approval.

Actions

  • Search all matching orders on the server; use the page-size selector and numbered pagination. Search and page state are kept in the URL.
  • Create one or more PO lines.
  • Submit a complete draft for approval.
  • Print the saved PO or open its revision history.
  • Open Purchase registers for full Excel exports or A3/A4 PDFs. Wide registers repeat document identity across column sections so every field stays readable.

Control checks

  • Confirm the supplier, category and plant before submission.
  • Confirm each line’s saved unit, rate per unit and physical count. KG and PCS totals are separate; cancelled and rejected orders do not add to open requirement.
  • Verify the current revision is approved before asking stores to receive it.

Expected outputs

  • Automatic PO number
  • Approved revision
  • Printable saved PO

05 / Measured kg and physical identity

Manual and PO-Linked Inward Guide

Workspace: /purchase/inward

Open Stores → Goods inward. Choose vendor and either an approved system PO or Manual GRN without PO. Enter multiple materials with one measured and labelled lot per physical reel or coil.

Receipt to usable stock

  1. Choose receipt source

    Select vendor, then approved PO and material lines. For a manual GRN, select materials directly and explain why no PO exists; a separate checker must approve it.

  2. Record invoice

    Enter invoice number, date, quantity and rate, or mark invoice pending to save held physical stock.

  3. Weigh every lot

    Enter or paste vendor reel number, net kg, measured width and optional gross/tare for every physical reel or coil, then review the staged rows.

  4. Post once

    The server rechecks balance and creates one GRN, one stock identity and one immutable label record per physical unit.

Field rules

  • Sum of individual net weights is the received quantity in kg. Add another material for mixed loads.
  • Tab moves between lot fields. Enter advances through inputs; Enter at the last lot field appends a new row. Paste columns: vendor number, net kg, width mm, form, gross kg, tare kg, vendor batch.
  • One reel or coil equals one lot, one AT number and one saved label; label reprints never create stock.
  • Invoice-pending and any rate or quantity difference stay commercially held until an authorized decision.
  • Measured width outside the approved PO width tolerance opens a separate specification case with lot-level evidence.
  • Gross minus tare must equal entered net kg within weighing precision when all three are supplied.

Actions

  • Preview controls before posting.
  • Validate the entered receipt, review every result and use Post goods inward once. Changing a field invalidates the old preview and requires validation again.
  • Prepare the saved AT label batch, then open the 4 × 2 inch PDF and print at actual size. Set copies and enter a reason for a reprint; reprinting never creates stock.
  • Open Stores → GRN register and labels to attach invoices, pass or hold QC, approve manual receipts or reprint labels.

Control checks

  • Verify total kg does not exceed the open PO line.
  • Verify each vendor reel number is unique for that supplier.
  • Resolve QC and commercial holds independently.

Expected outputs

  • Manual or PO-linked GRN
  • Physical lot identities
  • Saved labels
  • Invoice comparison cases

Related workspaces: /purchase · /purchase/discrepancies · /purchase/registers · /inventory/genealogy

06 / Commercial exception control

Invoice Difference and Debit Note Guide

Workspace: /purchase/discrepancies

Track each invoice rate, quantity or specification difference without changing the approved PO, hiding favorable variances, or mixing a stock decision with a financial claim.

Difference to settlement

  1. Review case

    Compare the receipt, invoice and approved revision at the saved allocation quantity.

  2. Decide stock

    Accept the difference or explicitly release material while the claim remains open; QC is still independent.

  3. Open claim

    Select positive rate differences for one vendor and create a numbered debit-note draft.

  4. Approve and settle

    Submit, approve, issue and record corrected invoice, credit note, adjustment or write-off references.

Field rules

  • Positive and favorable differences remain separate.
  • Quantity differences are visible but do not become a fabricated rate claim.
  • A receipt maker cannot approve their own commercial exception.
  • Partial settlement leaves the remaining claim open.

Actions

  • Record an audit reason for every decision.
  • Create one debit note per vendor.
  • Print or export the saved claim record.

Control checks

  • Do not clear QC by accepting a price.
  • Do not change PO or invoice evidence to force a match.
  • Confirm settlement amount never exceeds open claim value.

Expected outputs

  • Difference history
  • Stock release decision
  • Debit-note register
  • Settlement balance

07 / Calendar planning

Monthly RM Scheduler Guide

Workspace: /purchase/scheduler

Build a date-by-material kg plan from live stock, demand and open PO supply; review spreadsheet imports before they become plan entries or PO drafts.

Target to PO draft

  1. Review open sales demand

    Refresh material requirements from approved open sales orders and the existing BOM calculator. Unreleased units are reduced by accepted allocated finished goods before BOM calculation. Active jobs use frozen BOMs less item-specific net issues. Missing recipes, material mappings or shared consumption attribution block automatic MRP.

  2. Review MRP

    The server shows usable opening stock, committed PO supply, net need, rounding and data gaps.

  3. Place entries

    Choose a date on the monthly calendar, enter arrival kg and assign a vendor. Stage suggested shortages, distribute a monthly target, or switch to the workbook grid. Export Excel or print the month.

  4. Approve and convert

    Submit the plan, approve its version, review vendor assignments, then create traceable PO drafts.

Field rules

  • Choose the worksheet and KG or MT unit. Only the first daily RM block imports; lower finished-goods tables are excluded. Map each material header or explicitly exclude a helper column. Duplicate material/date cells are summed before staging.
  • Unknown item/vendor codes remain blocked rows; no fuzzy master creation.
  • Physical count never calculates actual kg.
  • Only approved plan entries convert, and retries cannot duplicate POs.

Actions

  • Import the supplied workbook or canonical template.
  • Enter kg directly in calendar cells.
  • Run explainable MRP.
  • Generate PO drafts grouped by vendor and series.

Control checks

  • Do not double count opening stock and live stock.
  • Keep held stock outside usable supply.
  • Review MOQ and order-multiple overage before conversion.

Expected outputs

  • Versioned month plan
  • MRP explanation
  • Vendor grouping
  • Linked PO drafts

Related workspaces: /analytics/mrp · /purchase/new · /purchase · /inventory/stock-alert-policies

08 / Versioned stock thresholds

Stock Alert Policy Guide

Workspace: /inventory/stock-alert-policies

Define the single effective safety, reorder and target stock policy used by alerts, MRP and procurement planning for each material and plant.

Policy to alert episode

  1. Create draft

    Select a material and enter safety, reorder, target, recovery, lead time, MOQ and order multiple in kg.

  2. Validate order

    Target must be at least reorder, and reorder at least safety.

  3. Activate

    An authorized activation supersedes the previous policy version while preserving history.

  4. Work alerts

    Evaluate usable stock, then acknowledge, assign, snooze or follow the source shortage to a PO draft.

Field rules

  • Policies are plant and item scoped.
  • QC-held, blocked and commercial-held stock is excluded from usable stock.
  • Acknowledging an alert never changes stock.
  • Recovery uses the saved recovery margin to prevent repeated alert noise.

Actions

  • Save policy draft.
  • Activate a reviewed version.
  • Evaluate alerts.
  • Open the alert inbox.

Control checks

  • Confirm units are kg.
  • Confirm recipients and cooldown.
  • Review existing open PO coverage before buying again.

Expected outputs

  • Effective policy version
  • Deduplicated alert episode
  • MRP threshold input

Control checks

  • Record a clear change reason.

Expected outputs

Plan released demand by machine and shift using meter-based capacity and drag-down scheduling controls.

Planner execution flow

  • Capacity shown on cards should match machine settings.

15 / Stage and final inspection

Quality Control Guide

Workspace: /quality

Run basic QC at each process completion and full product specification QC before finished goods are released.

QC decision flow

  1. Stage check

    Record quick process QC after each completion.

  2. Final spec

    Check length, OD, color, finish, packing, and customer-specific requirements.

  3. Decision

    Pass, hold, rework, or reject with remarks and evidence.

  4. Release

    Only passed stock moves to unrestricted FG or dispatch.

Field rules

  • QC decisions should reference job card, batch, or FG lot.
  • Hold and reject reasons must be clear enough for audit.
  • Final QC should compare against the saved specification, not memory.
  • Mark an item or stage criterion Critical: cannot be waived before approval when required by the client. Failed critical checks cannot be released by a reason, administrator concession or stage override. Existing lots and jobs keep their frozen rules.
  • Editing an approved item profile opens a new draft. Save it for review and approve separately; receipts continue using the prior approved revision until then.

Actions

  • Create stage QC checks during supervisor completion.
  • Create final product QC before dispatch.
  • Use holds to block bad stock without losing traceability.

Control checks

  • Confirm QC hold prevents dispatch.
  • Review repeated defects through reports.
  • Attach remarks for every failed or reworked quantity.

Expected outputs

  • QC pass
  • QC hold
  • Rework or reject trail

16 / FG to customer

Dispatch Guide

Workspace: /logistics/dispatch

Pack, verify, and dispatch finished goods only after production, inventory, and quality controls are complete.

Dispatch handoff flow

  1. Pick FG

    Select finished goods lot or job card output ready for dispatch.

  2. Verify QC

    Confirm no active hold remains and a current complete final QC inspection has passed. Packing measurements alone do not release the job.

  3. Prepare shipment

    Select the remaining quantity to ship, review packing and customer details, then enter vehicle, transporter and LR information. Phone screens show the same fields in stacked sections.

  4. Post dispatch

    Seal once. Only this shipment's quantity moves out; sales fulfillment and remaining FG update. Open the sealed shipment to print its challan. A retry of the same request does not post stock twice.

Field rules

  • Dispatch should reference customer order and FG lot.
  • Blocked or QC-held stock must not ship.
  • Packing box color label comes from the packing master color field.
  • The challan and inventory movement use the same India business date, frozen when the shipment is sealed.

Actions

  • Create dispatch from ready FG.
  • Print dispatch document for shipment.
  • Review dispatch history for customer follow-up.

Control checks

  • Confirm customer, quantity, and lot before posting.
  • Verify available FG balance after partial dispatch.
  • Check transport details before final print.

Expected outputs

  • Dispatch document
  • FG stock out
  • Customer shipment trail
