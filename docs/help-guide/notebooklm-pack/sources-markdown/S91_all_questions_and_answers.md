# Hari Om TubeOS: all common questions and answers (collected from every chapter)
Accurate as of 8 November 2026.


## From chapter 00: Welcome to Hari Om TubeOS: the big picture

**Q: Why does my sidebar look different from my colleague's?** A: Each role sees only its pages. See the role table in Getting started.

**Q: The plan says "not planned yet" but the operator already logged a run. Is that wrong?** A: No. Planning never blocks. The run is kept and flagged as before planning.

**Q: A reading was outside the band at Oven. Is the card stopped?** A: No. Job card readings are flagged, not held. The card carries on and its boxes still ship; managers see the flag.

**Q: What is a part box?** A: The last box of a job card, packed when Process closes, holding the pcs that did not fill a full box. Its label says PART BOX.

**Q: Winding could not make the full quantity. What happens to the order?** A: The plant manager closes Winding short with a reason. The unmade pcs go back to the order line at once, and Sales releases them again or short closes the order.

**Q: A reel failed incoming QC. Can the store issue it?** A: No. A held lot cannot be issued until QC re-checks it or the plant head concedes it.

**Q: Why is a GRN in January called P/26/…?** A: GRN numbers follow the financial year (April to March). January to March 2027 belongs to 2026-27.

**Q: Where do I see everything waiting for me?** A: The **Inbox** (sidebar Overview) and the bell in the header.


## From chapter 01: Getting started

**Q: A form asks "Choose why". What do I pick?** A: The reason that fits best from the list. If none fits, pick the OTHER code and write a short note. Ask a Plant Manager, Admin or the Owner to add a new code if it comes up often.

**Q: I scanned a box label. What opens?** A: The box page: its pcs, where it is (packed, dispatched or kept as surplus), its QC flags and its job card. Phone cameras and the in-app **Scan** both work.

**Q: I cannot find a page my colleague uses.** A: It belongs to a role your login does not have. Ask the Owner or Admin to add the role in System › Users.

**Q: The plant switcher is missing.** A: Only the Owner and Admin can switch plants. Your plant comes from your login.

**Q: The page says "pick one plant" but I only have one plant.** A: You are probably in **All Visible Plants** (Owner/Admin). Choose Plant A or Plant B in the switcher.

**Q: Text looks wrong in dark mode.** A: Click the sun/moon button to go back to light, or set Appearance › **Light**. Tell the Admin which page looked wrong.

**Q: My phone keeps signing me out.** A: That is the 15-minute safety rule. Signing in again returns you to the same page.

**Q: What does the amber "Close Sep" chip mean?** A: September has ended but has not been closed. The Owner closes it in Month-end close after the count is approved.

**Q: I switched role but the sidebar did not change.** A: The sidebar always shows the pages of all your roles. The role switcher changes your start page and work queues.


## From chapter 02: Dashboard and inbox

**Q: A tile says "Not reported". Is the figure zero?** A: No. That source did not answer. Press refresh in a minute; the list page shows live status meanwhile.

**Q: Why do I not see Company KPIs?** A: Only the Owner has the KPI and Action center tabs. Other roles see figures for their own work.

**Q: Why does clicking Dashboard take me to the QC desk?** A: The Quality Control role's dashboard is the QC desk.

**Q: I marked a notification read by mistake.** A: Click its read/unread button to **Mark as unread**.

**Q: The Inbox says 2,000 unread. Do I have to open each?** A: No. Work the **Needs action** view, then press **Mark all read**.

**Q: A notification has no button.** A: It is for information, or your role cannot do that action. Open it to read.

**Q: I pressed Make debit note in the Inbox. Where is the debit note?** A: It is a draft in Purchasing › Debit notes. The notification shows its number. Submit, approve and issue it as usual.

**Q: The button said it did not go through.** A: Read the red message (for example the case changed or your role may not do it). Use "Try … again" or open the page from the link.

**Q: Purchase lanes say "Select a plant to see this queue".** A: You are in All Visible Plants. Choose Plant A or Plant B in the header.


## From chapter 03: Using TubeOS on a phone

**Q: The Camera app shows the QR but nothing opens.** A: Tap the link banner that appears. If there is no link, it is an old label: type its number on the Scan page or ask the store to reprint it.

**Q: "We could not read this label".** A: The number is not in your plant or the connection dropped. Check the network and scan again, or type the number.

**Q: Log output is grey.** A: Read the small text under the button: the stage or card is closed, or your role cannot log runs.

**Q: My phone signed me out in the middle of a run.** A: Sign in again; the card reopens and your typed draft is offered back.

**Q: I scanned a reel and got a lot card, not the issue page.** A: Either your role does not issue stock, or the reel is not cleared (the card says "Waiting for incoming QC" or "Failed incoming QC").

**Q: Do I need to choose a shift when issuing?** A: No. Shift and date come from the clock when you press Issue.

**Q: Can I use a handheld scanner?** A: Yes. In-app scan boxes read new links and older labels alike.


## From chapter 04: Sales orders

**Q: Why can't I approve my own order?** A: Maker-checker. Press **Send for approval**; another user approves. Only the Owner and Admin may approve their own.

**Q: Release lines is grey.** A: Hover over it: "Approve the order first", "Lift the customer hold to release", "The order is closed" or "All lines are already released to job cards".

**Q: The release dialog says "Pick the parchment colour…" on a plain line.** A: Every job card needs a colour to wind; pick one now.

**Q: I want to cancel but only Short close is offered.** A: Something is already released or dispatched. Short close drops the rest; released cards finish.

**Q: The reason I need is not in the list.** A: Pick the OTHER code and write a note, and ask the Owner or Admin to add it in **Order close reasons** (for the Resume list, in Masters › Reason codes › Order hold lifted).

**Q: Why does my order show pcs "not released" again after release?** A: A job card gave them back: a stage closed short, or the card was force-closed, cancelled or kept boxes as surplus. Release them again or short close the order.

**Q: Can I undo a Resume?** A: Put the order on customer hold again with a reason. Both steps stay in the hold history.

**Q: Where did the Order tracker, Pending orders and Order audit pages go?** A: They are the Orders list (tiles and tabs) and the Order page's History tab. Old links still work.

**Q: The order shows "No date". Is that a problem?** A: No. Delivery date is optional; add it on the Deliveries tab when the customer confirms.

**Q: I entered the wrong PO number after release.** A: Use **Change PO (once)**. After that it is locked; ask the Owner for a decision.


## From chapter 05: Specifications

**Q: My spec does not appear on the sales order.** A: It is not approved yet, or it is obsolete. Approve it; drafts are listed under "Not approved yet — approve in Specifications first".

**Q: Approve & Go Live is grey.** A: Hover over it for the reason: not Owner/Admin, no plant selected, not submitted, or blockers left.

**Q: The list shows "Needs attention" for Monsoon.** A: Open **Readiness** to see what is missing (for example the Monsoon recipe is not confirmed).

**Q: If I change the recipe, do running job cards change?** A: No. Each card keeps the recipe and tolerances it was released with.

**Q: Why does release say the spec is not ready?** A: The active season's recipe or QC setup has blockers. Fix them on the spec, or ask the Owner about the season.

**Q: Can Planners or Sales edit a spec?** A: No. Only the Owner and Admin. Others use approved specs.

**Q: Why can't I approve my new spec? It says Packing.** A: Pick a box from the Packaging master and type a whole qty per box in the Packing section.

**Q: The box we use is not in the list.** A: Add it in Masters › Packaging (Boxes) with its size and weight per box, then pick it. On Packing setup, missing box codes are listed with **Add box**.

**Q: Does Packing setup need the spec to be approved again?** A: No. Only the packing changes, as a packing amendment with your name. The approved version stays live.

**Q: I changed the qty per box. Do running job cards change?** A: Open cards that have not packed any box yet take the new packing. A card that already packed boxes keeps its packing.

**Q: What is fadda?** A: The sheet put inside the box (putha). It is a Packaging master of kind Fadda.

**Q: What is the 12.5%?** A: The plant default allowance for adhesive and parchment together on top of the paper; parchment takes 1.5% and adhesive the rest. Change it with **Edit defaults** (plant level) or on the spec.


## From chapter 06: A day in the life

**Q: I am new. Which page do I open first?** A: Your start page (it opens after sign-in), then the Inbox.

**Q: Two people do my job. Do we share work?** A: Yes. Queues and lists are per plant and role, not per person.

**Q: I cover two roles.** A: Ask the Admin to add both roles to your login and use the role switcher.

**Q: Who do I ask when a button is grey?** A: Read its reason first. If it is a role or plant reason, ask the Owner or Admin.

**Q: Can I fix a mistake myself?** A: Usually yes, with a reason: edit the run, change the colour on the order, raise a stock correction. Approved and closed records need the Owner.


## From chapter 10: Job cards

**Q: Why is Log output greyed out?** A: Hover over it. Common reasons: "Card is closed", "Old entry form — use the Old form tab", "No open stage takes runs", or "Your role cannot log runs".

**Q: The card is not planned. Can the operator still log a run?** A: Yes. The run saves and is flagged "run before planning". Planning is only the plan; the job card log is the truth.

**Q: What does "Flagged · 3 runs" mean in the QC column?** A: Three runs have at least one reading outside its band. They were accepted and not held. Click the flag to open the card's QC tab.

**Q: What is the difference between a flag and a hold?** A: A flag is information; nothing stops. A hold is placed on purpose on the Quality pages by QC, the plant head, Owner or Admin; it stops the next stage and dispatch until the plant head, Owner or Admin releases it. QC readings never make a hold by themselves.

**Q: Winding made less than planned. Do I have to close the whole card?** A: No. Close Winding short with a reason. The unmade pcs go back to the order, Oven and Process carry on with what Winding passed on, and the card finishes normally.

**Q: Where do the returned pcs go?** A: To the sales order line as unreleased pcs. Sales can release them again on a new card or short close the order with a reason.

**Q: I closed a stage short by mistake. Can I undo it?** A: The Owner or Admin can reopen the stage with a reason. The returned pcs come back to the card only if the order has not released them again or been short-closed.

**Q: Why are 7 pcs in a box of 48?** A: That is the part box: the pcs left when Process closed. Its label says PART BOX. It ships like any other box.

**Q: Why has no box been packed yet, although Process logged pcs?** A: The run's QC readings are not done (2 per required check), or the spec has no qty per box. Log the readings, or ask the Owner or Admin to set the packing on **Packing setup**.

**Q: I printed a box label twice. Is there extra stock now?** A: No. A print is only counted on the box. Stock comes only from packing.

**Q: Why can't I void this Process run?** A: Its pcs are already in a box. Correct it another way with the supervisor; the box and its stock stay as they are.

**Q: Why can't I close the stage?** A: The close dialog says why. Usually QC readings are short ("I.D. 1/2"), runs are still with the supervisor, or no reason was chosen for a short close. Only Plant Manager, Owner or Admin can confirm a close.

**Q: What does "Missed slot ×2" mean?** A: The card was planned twice and nothing was logged within 36 hours after each shift, so it went back to the planner queue. Plan it again, or log the run if it did happen.

**Q: What is a "count gap"?** A: On Process, the pieces counted (made + cutting loss + held) do not match bamboo × pcs per bamboo. The run is saved and flagged for the owner; nothing has to be written for it.

**Q: How do I split, edit or cancel a card?** A: Click **Manage card**. That opens the Job card manager (see the next chapter).


## From chapter 11: Job card manager

**Q: Why is Edit greyed out?** A: The card is already on the board or running. Take it back to the queue on the planner, or split the unstarted part into a new card.

**Q: When do I use Split into a new card and when Split plan?** A: Split into a new card when part of the order needs its own card (own print, QC, dispatch). Split plan when the same card should run over more shifts, days or machines.

**Q: Force close is locked on my card. Why?** A: It is a batch-entry card with runs logged. Open the card page and use **Close stage** on the stage that cannot finish; a short close needs a reason code and sends the balance back to the order.

**Q: What does Back to order show?** A: The pcs this card gave back to the sales order (force close, cancel or a stage short close) that Sales has not released again yet. Sales releases them again or short closes the order on the order page.

**Q: The reason I need is not in the list.** A: Pick the OTHER code of that list and write a note, and ask a Plant Manager, Admin or the Owner to add the code in Masters › Reason codes.

**Q: Can I undo a cancel?** A: No. Release the quantity again from the sales order to make a new card.

**Q: Who gets told about an emergency insert?** A: Everyone affected is notified, and each card that had to move gets the move in its history.

**Q: Does closing a stage still need a supervisor's name?** A: No. Plant Manager, Owner or Admin closes it, and that login is recorded.

**Q: The new split card is not on the board. Is that normal?** A: Yes. A split card starts in the planner queue, unplaced. Use **Place it on the planner board**.


## From chapter 12: Planner

**Q: Why can't I drag this card onto the Oven board?** A: Its Winding has no slot yet. Plan Winding first; the card shows in **Waiting for the previous plan** with a lock.

**Q: The floor logged a run but the card was not planned. Is that wrong?** A: No. It is allowed and flagged "Run before planning". The overview shows the slot as **Logged before planning**.

**Q: What does "Missed slot ×2" mean?** A: The card was planned twice and nothing was logged within 36 hours after each shift ended. Re-plan it, or have the floor log the run if it happened.

**Q: What is the difference between Split plan and Split into a new card?** A: Split plan keeps one card and cuts its plan into two time slots. Split into a new card (Job card manager) creates a separate card with its own number.

**Q: The capacity bar says 120%. Will it stop me?** A: No. You are asked first; **Schedule and split** keeps what fits and moves the rest to the next shift.

**Q: Why do the queue panel bars not match machine use?** A: They compare queued load only, including missed slots. They do not measure machine utilization.

**Q: Where did the Order tracker go?** A: Old /planning/tracker links open the Orders list with the matching filter. Missed slots are on the Planning overview.


## From chapter 13: Log output and QC entry

**Q: The scan opened "That QR is on a material or tool label". What now?** A: You scanned a reel, lot or tool label. Scan the QR on the printed job card.

**Q: It is 2 a.m. Why does the form say yesterday's date?** A: The run belongs to Shift B, which started yesterday at 20:00. That is correct.

**Q: Do I have to fill start and end time?** A: No. They are optional and never stop a save.

**Q: My reading is outside the band. Will the card stop?** A: No. It is saved and flagged, also on the boxes of that run. Only a hold placed on purpose on the Quality pages stops a card.

**Q: My name is not in the Operator list.** A: Your department in Masters › Employees is not this stage, or you are inactive. Ask the Owner or Admin to set your department (for example PROCESS).

**Q: Save stays grey after I typed Rejected.** A: Pick a code under **Why rejected?**. With RJ-OTHER, also write a note of at least 5 characters.

**Q: Why did Log QC on Process make new boxes?** A: Pcs go into boxes once their readings are done. Print the labels from the card's Dispatch tab.

**Q: Why can't the next stage take my batch?** A: Its QC is not done yet. Add at least 2 readings for every required check on that run, or ask the supervisor to override with a reason.

**Q: I saved a run but it does not count.** A: It is with the supervisor. A plant manager, Owner or Admin must submit it.

**Q: I typed the wrong quantity on a submitted run.** A: Ask the supervisor to void it with a reason, then log it again. If the next stage already used that output, it cannot be voided.

**Q: Where is the old Supervisor entry page?** A: It now opens Floor entry. Use Log output.


## From chapter 14: Quality

**Q: Why can't Store issue this reel?** A: Either QC has not weighed and accepted it yet, or it is held (Hold, Reject, or a reading out of band). QC must weigh and accept it, re-check it, the plant head concede it, or Store return it.

**Q: Do I have to enter GSM, moisture and the other checks?** A: No. Every check is optional now. Only the actual weight is needed, and only to Accept. If you do enter a reading and it is out of band, the reel is held.

**Q: I typed 11750 instead of 1175 and saved.** A: Click the pencil (**Re-weigh**), type 1175, pick WC-TYPO and save. The bill's weight check is worked out again.

**Q: The scale shows 30 kg less than the bill. Does stock go down?** A: No. Stock, labels and month close stay on the bill weight. The difference goes to the bill's weight check, and from there to the Owner or a debit note.

**Q: A reel received last week is still on hold. Why can't I release it?** A: Lots received before go-live of weighing (7 Nov 2026) must be weighed too. Type its actual weight and accept it.

**Q: A floor reading is out of band. Must I hold the card?** A: No. It is flagged, not held, and the boxes still ship. Place a hold only if you decide the material must stop.

**Q: Why did no hold appear when final QC failed?** A: Job card QC only flags. Holds on job cards are placed by people, on purpose. Incoming (GRN) lots are still held automatically when out of band.

**Q: The hold reason I need is not in the list.** A: Pick QH-OTHER and write a note, and ask a Plant Manager, Admin or the Owner to add the code in Masters › Reason codes.

**Q: Who can release a job-card hold?** A: Only the plant head (Plant Manager), Owner or Admin.

**Q: Where do I change the GSM band for one paper?** A: In Masters (the band button on the paper row, or Masters › Inward QC tolerances). QC only lists the checks.

**Q: I edited a stage rule. Why do old cards still use the old band?** A: Cards freeze their rules at release. Only cards released after your save use the new version.

**Q: What does "Recorded only — no limit" mean?** A: The reading is kept but never judged in or out of band.

**Q: A complaint's dispatch number is not found.** A: The complaint is still logged, marked unverified. Check the DC number with Dispatch.

**Q: Where are quality trends?** A: **Analytics** (Reports › Quality analytics): pass rates by stage and material, flagged readings, holds and complaints.


## From chapter 15: Month-end close

**Q: Why does every item show "Waiting for count"?** A: The month-end count is not approved yet. Used per count is known only after the Owner approves the count.

**Q: The close button is grey. Why?** A: Hover over it: a checklist step is open, or you are not the Owner. The first open step is marked.

**Q: The operator cannot save a run for last month.** A: That month is closed. Ask the Owner to re-open it, enter the run, then close it again.

**Q: Why is spec 0 for an item we used?** A: Spec is posted when Process closes on a card. If no card using it closed Process in the month, spec is 0. Also check "Spec items not linked to a stock item".

**Q: Are usage limits the same as QC tolerances?** A: No. Usage limits are for month-end only. QC bands are in Quality and Masters.

**Q: I changed a limit. Did August change?** A: Only if August is still open. Closed months keep their own limits.

**Q: What does "Legacy" mean in time reconciliation?** A: The stage record has no card-time source of its own. It is information only.


## From chapter 16: Dispatch

**Q: The watchman says the truck left, but the challan still says Not out of gate.** A: The goods-out entry is waiting under **Gone out at the gate**. Store picks it, ticks the challan, confirms the Tally bill number and clicks **Link challan**.

**Q: I linked the wrong challan or typed the wrong Tally number.** A: Ask the plant head, Owner or Admin to **Unlink** it with a reason, then link it again correctly.

**Q: Does the watchman type the Tally bill number?** A: No. He only photographs the Tally bill and our challan and types the vehicle number. Store (or Dispatch) types or confirms the Tally number when linking.

**Q: The chip says Photos missing.** A: The gate phone has not finished uploading. Ask the watchman to keep the phone on the network. If the photo was lost on the phone, tell the plant head.

**Q: Why is there no Make challan button on my card?** A: Read the state: "Packing boxes" with no box in stock yet, "Waiting for the stock posting" or "On QC hold". The button appears only when boxes can ship and your role can prepare challans.

**Q: Can I ship before Process is closed?** A: Yes. Every box that is packed and in stock can ship. The last pcs follow in the part box after Process closes.

**Q: Can I take some pcs out of a box?** A: No. A box always ships whole. If a box no longer fits in what can ship, keep it with **Retain surplus FG** once the rest is shipped.

**Q: A box is QC flagged. Can I still ship it?** A: Yes. Flags do not block. Only a hold placed on purpose on the card blocks sealing.

**Q: The scan says the box is not on this job card.** A: The label belongs to another card. Make that card's challan separately; one challan is one job card.

**Q: The seal says the plant GSTIN is missing.** A: Ask an Owner or Admin to fill it in **Plants** (System). Then press **Reload** on the challan.

**Q: Can Sales seal a challan?** A: No. Sales can view and print sealed challans. Dispatch, Owner or Admin seal.

**Q: I sealed the wrong boxes.** A: A sealed challan cannot be changed. Speak to the Owner or Admin about a correction; do not make a second challan for the same boxes.

**Q: Why can only 300 pcs ship when the order released 400?** A: Winding closed short and gave 100 pcs back to the order. The card may ship only what is still released on it; Sales handles the 100 pcs on the order.

**Q: The net weight is empty.** A: The spec has no tube weight. Type the net weight in kg.


## From chapter 20: Purchase orders, PO approvals and requisitions

**Q: Why can't I see the New purchase order button?** A: Only Store, Plant Manager, Owner and Admin make POs. A Planner proposes arrivals on the RM inward calendar; when the Owner approves them, the draft PO is made automatically.

**Q: Where do I type the PO number?** A: You don't. The system gives the next RP-PM or OT number when you save the draft, and it never changes, even after revisions.

**Q: Where is the "Incoming QC required" tick?** A: It was removed. Every PO line now needs incoming QC, and QC checks each delivery against the material standard.

**Q: A scheduled draft says "Rate needed". What do I do?** A: Click **Fill the rate**, enter the vendor's rate and save. If the Owner did not make this change himself, the PO then goes to him for approval before it can be sent.

**Q: The Owner rejected my PO. Can I fix it?** A: Yes. Open it from **Sent back by Owner**, read the reason in the history, click **Edit / vendor change**, fix it and submit again. Or cancel it.

**Q: Can an Admin approve a PO they made?** A: Yes. The Owner and an Admin may approve their own PO; the history records "approved by the same person". RM arrivals and requisitions are approved by the Owner only.

**Q: Can I receive goods on a PO that is still waiting approval?** A: No. Goods inward lists only approved POs with an open balance. Ask the Owner or an Admin to approve it.

**Q: The vendor will not send the last 120 kg. How do I close the PO?** A: The Plant Manager or Owner picks a close reason (for example PC-SUPPLIER-CANNOT) and clicks **Short-close remaining quantities**.

**Q: I changed a sent PO. Does the vendor get it automatically?** A: No. The PO shows "Revision 2 approved; send it again". Use **Send again**.


## From chapter 21: RM inward calendar and expected arrivals

**Q: I proposed 500 kg but the balance did not change. Why?** A: A proposal is not counted until the Owner approves it. Look at **If proposals approved** in the material sheet to see the effect.

**Q: Reels are in the store but the calendar still says short. Why?** A: Reels waiting for incoming QC count from tomorrow, not today. If QC rejects them they never count. Ask QC to inspect them.

**Q: Why can't I approve arrivals?** A: Only the Owner approves arrivals. Admin and the desk can pick a vendor for an arrival so it is ready for him.

**Q: The Owner approved arrivals but nothing came to the vendor.** A: Approval makes a draft PO. The desk must still open **Scheduled drafts to send** and click **Check & send**.

**Q: What does "Need is not fully known" mean?** A: Some order lines could not be turned into material need, or a material has no stock item linked. Click **Details**; link missing materials in Inventory › Items.

**Q: An arrival shows "Overdue". Is it still counted?** A: Yes, an overdue arrival that is not received is counted on today. Chase the vendor and use **Change date**, or make the GRN if the goods came.

**Q: A row in Expected arrivals shows "Add date" instead of "Confirm".** A: That PO line has no dated delivery row yet. **Add date** creates one with the date the vendor gives.

**Q: Why is my approved PO not in Expected arrivals?** A: Only approved and partly received POs with an open quantity are listed. A draft or a PO waiting approval is not expected yet.


## From chapter 22: Invoice differences, debit notes and purchase reports

**Q: QC weighed the bill 30 kg short. Does stock go down by 30 kg?** A: No. Stock stays on the bill weight. Only the difference is claimed, through a debit note.

**Q: I skipped a weight debit note by mistake.** A: A skipped bill no longer shows **Make debit note**; the skip and its reason stay on the case. Speak to the Owner or Admin.

**Q: Why does the weight case say "Waits for invoice"?** A: The GRN was posted with the invoice pending. The debit-note draft is made as soon as the invoice is attached in the GRN register.

**Q: Can I change the 20 and 50 kg limits?** A: Only the Owner, in System › Plants (Weight check of a bill). The limits saved on a bill when it was weighed stay with that bill.

**Q: The vendor's rate is ₹0.10 higher. Do I really need a case?** A: Yes. Any rate difference opens a case. Accept it with a reason code (for example ID-TAX for GST rounding) if the extra is agreed.

**Q: Why is the Claim box greyed out on my row?** A: Only positive **Rate**, **Quantity** or **Quality** rows that are still open and have the invoice attached can be claimed. Rows with "Invoice pending" become claimable after the invoice is attached in the GRN register.

**Q: Can the reels be used while the rate case is open?** A: Yes, once incoming QC passes them. Invoice and rate questions never hold paper back.

**Q: I made the GRN. Why can't I accept its difference?** A: The person who made the receipt cannot decide its own exception. Ask the plant head or Accounts.

**Q: Does accepting a difference change the PO rate for next time?** A: No. The PO stays as approved. Only the stock cost of that receipt line follows the invoice rate.

**Q: Does issuing a debit note mean the money is recovered?** A: No. Issued means the claim was sent. Record each payment or credit with **Record settlement**; the note is **Settled** only when nothing is open.

**Q: Which register do I give the auditor for purchases?** A: The **GRN receipt register** (one row per received line) and the **Inward and lot register** (one row per reel or coil), as Full Excel.


## From chapter 23: Goods inward, GRN register and labels

**Q: The vendor sent only the challan. Can I still receive?** A: Yes. Tick **Challan received · invoice pending**, type the challan rate if you have it, and attach the invoice later in the GRN register. The reels are not held for the invoice.

**Q: Why doesn't my PO appear in Approved purchase order?** A: Only approved POs of that vendor with an open quantity are listed. It may still be waiting approval, fully received or closed.

**Q: Do I enter gross weight and tare?** A: No. Enter only the net kg of each reel or coil (paper alone).

**Q: I typed the wrong weight and already posted. How do I fix it?** A: A posted GRN cannot be edited. Raise a stock correction with the reason "Wrong GRN weight"; the Owner approves it (see the stock counts chapter).

**Q: Why does the inward number start with IN and not P?** A: P is used only when every line is paper, A only when every line is adhesive. Anything else, or a mix, uses IN.

**Q: It is January 2027. Why is the number still P/26/…?** A: The number follows the financial year, April to March. 26 means 2026-27; it changes to P/27/001 on 1 April 2027.

**Q: The label got wet. How do I print a new one?** A: Open the receipt in the GRN register, type a **Reprint reason** and click **Prepare 1 label**. The reprint keeps the same AIT serial.

**Q: Why does the new reel say AIT 06281 and not AT-…?** A: New reels follow the plant's own AIT series. Old reels keep their AT numbers; both can be scanned and searched.

**Q: The label shows the vendor name, not the mill name.** A: The vendor has no **Mill / short name**. Ask the Owner or Admin to fill it in Masters › Vendors; the next print uses it.

**Q: Why does the GSM on the label equal the variety?** A: No GSM was measured at incoming QC yet, so the label prints the variety number, as the plant always has. Once QC measures it, a reprint shows the measured GSM.

**Q: Can I find a reel by typing its serial?** A: Yes. Type it any way (ait 6243, AIT-06243, AIT 6243-2); the search reads it as AIT 06243 or AIT 06243-2.

**Q: The bill was photographed at the gate. Do I still type the challan and invoice?** A: Yes. Pick the bill under **At the gate** and read the numbers from the photo beside the form. The photo is linked when you post.

**Q: The At the gate card says "Vendor not given".** A: The watchman did not pick a vendor. Pick the vendor on the form yourself.

**Q: Why does my GRN say No gate photo?** A: It was posted without a gate entry. If the watchman photographed the bill, click **Link gate entry** on the GRN.

**Q: Can the reels go to the floor before QC?** A: No. They show **Waiting for QC** until incoming QC passes them. Rate and invoice questions never hold them, but QC does.


## From chapter 24: Issue to the floor: reels, coils and materials

**Q: The label says AIT 06281 but the desk asks for an AT number. Which do I type?** A: Type what is on the label. New labels carry the AIT serial; older ones the AT number. Both are found.

**Q: Why isn't there a shift or date box?** A: The shift and date come from the clock when you press Issue. An issue at 01:30 on 7 Nov is Shift B of 6 Nov.

**Q: A reel is in the store but not in Ready for the floor. Why?** A: It has not passed incoming QC. It is under **Not cleared yet** with the reason. Ask QC to inspect it, or use **Inspect now** if you have QC access.

**Q: Can I issue half a reel?** A: No. A reel or coil goes to the floor whole. Material issue (adhesive, packing, parchment) is by quantity.

**Q: The floor did not use all the adhesive. Can I take it back into stock?** A: No. Nothing comes back from the floor; the stock count settles what was really used.

**Q: I issued the wrong reel. How do I undo it?** A: You cannot undo an issue. Issue the right reel. If the wrong one is still in the store, the next stock count shows it as "Issued, but found in store", and the Owner decides whether to add it back on QC hold.

**Q: I scanned a parchment coil on the reel desk and got a message.** A: Parchment is issued by colour and kg on the material desk. Click **Open material issue** in the message.

**Q: What does the RMIssue role allow?** A: Raw material issue only: issue reels, coils and materials to the floor, write slitting cards, read issue history and genealogy, and take part in stock counts. Its home page is Reel & coil issue.

**Q: What are "Older open issues"?** A: Reels issued to a machine before floor issue started. Close each once with **Used kg**, or **Back to stock, then issue to floor** to return it unchanged and issue it again. New issues never appear here.

**Q: Why does the desk say "Stock is still loading"?** A: You scanned before the stock list finished loading. The label is checked as soon as it is ready; you do not need to scan again.


## From chapter 25: Stock counts, stock book, locations and opening stock

**Q: I counted a rack correctly, so why does it show lots "not found"?** A: Rack done marks every lot the book expects on that rack and nobody scanned as not found. Use **Look again** before confirming. If the lot is on another rack, scan it there; it will show as **In the wrong place** and move on approval, not be written off.

**Q: A reel was issued to the floor but it is still lying in the store. Can I return it?** A: No. Nothing comes back from the floor. Scan it in a count; it shows as **Issued, but found in store**, and the Owner either leaves it as used or adds it as new stock on QC hold with a new label.

**Q: Why can't I start the month-end count?** A: Read the note on the card. The running month opens only in its last 3 days. A closed month, or a month before a closed month, cannot be counted. A spot count can run any time.

**Q: What is the difference between a whole-store spot count and the month-end count?** A: Both change stock when approved, but only the month-end count becomes the month's stock certificate, which Month-end close uses.

**Q: The Owner approved a count with a mistake. How do we undo it?** A: Approval cannot be undone. Fix it with a new spot count of that rack, or a correction for that lot.

**Q: Why did my correction post a different quantity from what I raised?** A: At approval the system re-bases it on the stock at that moment. If a count or another correction already changed that lot, only the remaining difference is posted, and the note on the line says so.

**Q: Why does the stock book say "Opens from the book on the 1st" and not from last month's close?** A: The previous month is not closed yet. After it is closed, the chip says "Opens from last month's close".

**Q: Why is the Value on a count shown in amber, with "with no rate"?** A: Some lines have no rate yet, usually because the GRN invoice is pending. Those lines are left out of the ₹ total, but their stock change still posts in full.

**Q: Can I scan an old AT label in a count?** A: Yes. Old AT numbers and new AIT serials are both found by scan or by typing ("ait 6243" finds AIT 06243).

**Q: Why doesn't the reel label say "QC hold"?** A: The reel label matches the plant's own label and does not print the QC state. Scan its QR to see whether it is cleared.

**Q: We forgot one rack at go-live. Can we still add it?** A: Yes, as a new batch on the same go-live date, until the go-live month is closed. After that, add the stock through a stock count.

**Q: Where does goods inward put stock if I do not choose a rack?** A: In the location marked **RM store · goods inward lands here**. The Owner or Admin can make another active store rack the RM store from its **More actions** menu.


## From chapter 26: Inventory, material items, manual FG, stock alerts and RM costing

**Q: Why does Stock on hand not include our cartons and cores?** A: That tile and the material chart add up only materials counted in kg. Pieces are shown separately, because kg and pcs are never added together.

**Q: I saved a stock alert policy but no alerts appear.** A: A draft does nothing. A different person must click **Activate as checker**, and then someone must click **Evaluate active policies now**.

**Q: I am the Plant Manager and drafted the policy myself. Why can't I activate it?** A: Policies use maker-checker. Ask another Plant Manager, the Owner or the Admin to activate it.

**Q: Stock went up but the alert is still open.** A: It recovers only above reorder + recovery margin, and only when Evaluate runs again. Held lots do not count as free stock until QC clears them.

**Q: Why do finished goods show many small lots like B2610315-001?** A: Each box from a job card is its own FG lot, named by its box code. That is how a challan takes exact boxes out of stock. Reprinting a box label never adds stock.

**Q: A customer sent back 500 tubes. Do I enter them in Manual FG?** A: No. The system refuses customer returns here. Record them in Quality > Customer complaints, so they are held for QC and linked to the customer and dispatch.

**Q: I posted manual FG twice by mistake.** A: If you gave the same Reference both times, the second post was ignored. If not, ask the Plant Manager to correct the extra stock; the post cannot be undone from this page.

**Q: Why can't I delete an old paper item?** A: It still has stock. Issue, count or correct the balance to zero first. Deleting only switches the item off; its history stays.

**Q: Does activating a new RM cost change what we paid on old POs?** A: No. It is a planning cost only. PO rates, invoice rates and the cost of lots already received are never changed.

**Q: Where does a new paper master show up as a stock item?** A: Automatically, with the same code, when the master is saved. You then set its reorder, safety and lead days under **Material items**.


## From chapter 27: Reports

**Q: Why does a tile say "Not reported"?** A: Either your role cannot read that source (pack figures need Owner, Admin, Planner or Plant Manager), or the source did not answer. Refresh after a minute; it never shows a false zero.

**Q: Why is machine utilization above 100%?** A: The load assigned to the machines is larger than the capacity set for them, often because capacity is entered in a different or smaller unit. Check the machine's capacity in System > Machines.

**Q: OTIF shows 0% but we delivered on time.** A: OTIF counts only closed (fully dispatched) orders with a due date. Few closed orders, or orders without a due date, give 0%.

**Q: Why are so many runs "Run before planning"?** A: The floor logged the run before the planner gave the stage a slot. The floor is never blocked by this; it only shows the planner where the plan lagged.

**Q: Why is every customer "at risk" in Customer 360?** A: A customer with no closed order in the window has OTIF 0, which is below 90%, so it shows as Watch until orders close on time.

**Q: The slitting wastage % looks low compared with the paper issued.** A: The % uses only reels whose slitting card is written. Write the pending cards to complete it.

**Q: Can I change the window on Operations command or Sales pulse?** A: No. Operations command is always the last 7 days and Sales pulse and Customer 360 the last 30 days. Use Throughput or Planning performance for other periods.

**Q: An operator is missing from Employee performance.** A: Only operators picked on floor logs in the period appear. Check the period and filters; runs logged before operators were asked for may have no operator.

**Q: Why are Winding and Process figures in different units?** A: Each stage counts in its own unit: bamboos at Winding and Oven, pieces at Process. The tiles keep them apart.

**Q: A closed order shows a reason I don't see in the list any more.** A: The reason was deactivated or renamed later. Records keep the label they were saved with.

**Q: Can I get the owner pack by email?** A: The system can send the owner pack every day (06:30 by default) through Background jobs. Ask the Admin whether it is switched on for your plant.


## From chapter 28: Masters

**Q: I deleted a customer by mistake. Is it gone?** A: No. Delete only deactivates it. Set Status to Inactive, find it and click **Activate**.

**Q: Why can't I change an order close reason's code?** A: Closed orders keep that code. Rename it instead, or deactivate it and add a new one.

**Q: I changed a paper's GSM. Why does an old spec still show the old value?** A: Saved recipe versions keep the values they were saved with. Save a new recipe version to use the new value.

**Q: The customer row says "No dispatch address". Does it matter?** A: Yes for dispatch. The challan prints the ship-to address from here. Edit the customer and fill it, or tick **Same as the billing address**.

**Q: I typed a GSTIN and the PAN changed by itself.** A: A GSTIN contains the PAN (characters 3 to 12) and the state code (first 2 digits), so the system fills them to match.

**Q: A new paper does not appear in Material items.** A: It is created automatically when the master is saved. Refresh Material items. If its code is already used by another type of material, the system reports a conflict instead; use a different code.

**Q: Where do I change who can log in?** A: Not here. Employees are shop-floor people for reports. Logins are under System > Users.

**Q: Why can't the Plant Manager edit papers?** A: Masters are Owner and Admin only, except Reason codes, which the Plant Manager may also change.

**Q: A reason picker says "No reasons are set up for this list".** A: That list has no active code in this plant. Add one in Reason codes (the picker links there).

**Q: An operator is missing from the Log output list.** A: Check their **Dept** and that they are active in Employees. Winding shows WINDER people only, Oven OVEN, Process PROCESS.

**Q: What is the mill name on a reel label?** A: The vendor's **Mill / short name** (for example AKSHAT). Fill it on the vendor; until then the label prints the vendor name.


## From chapter 29: System, audit, production season and the guide

**Q: I changed a person's role but they still see the old screens.** A: Saving signs them out. Ask them to sign in again.

**Q: Where do I change the 20 kg and 50 kg weight limits?** A: System › Plants, on the plant card, **Weight check of a bill**. Owner only.

**Q: The watchman can see other pages.** A: Check his login has only the **Watchman (gate)** role. With any other role added, he is no longer gate-only.

**Q: Can a user have two roles?** A: Yes. Tick both. Access adds up, and they can switch role from the top bar.

**Q: Why can't I tick "Allow all plants" for a Plant Manager?** A: All-plant access is only for Owner and Admin. Tick each plant they need under **Allowed plants** instead.

**Q: A person left the company. Do I delete them?** A: Untick **Account active**. They cannot sign in, and their history stays in the audit trail.

**Q: Why does the Users list show fewer people than I expect?** A: It shows users of the plant chosen in the top plant switcher. Switch to all plants to see everyone.

**Q: Why did the season switch refuse with "Season preview changed"?** A: Someone changed a spec or QC rule after you opened the preview. Click the season again to get a fresh preview.

**Q: Does switching the season change job cards already on the floor?** A: No. Released job cards keep the season and tolerances they were released with. Only new releases use the new season.

**Q: The daily summary did not arrive.** A: Check **Background jobs**. If it says "Run by the server's timer", the server sends it at 6:30 am. If an error shows under **What went wrong**, tell the Admin.

**Q: Where is the latest guide?** A: The Guide page in the app (Full handbook tab) and the same handbook on the sign-in screen link. The book icon on any page opens the guide for that page.

**Q: Our hand-printed labels reached AIT 06279. How do we carry on?** A: The Owner or Admin opens System > Plants, clicks **Set next number** on the plant card, types 6280 with a reason and saves. The next reel received gets AIT 06280.

**Q: Can we start the AIT series again mid-year?** A: No. After reels of the year have numbers, the next number can only go up. The series restarts at AIT 00001 by itself on 1 April.


## From chapter 31: Gate desk and visitors

**Q: Do I have to type the bill number or the Tally bill number at the gate?** A: No. The watchman only takes photos, taps the kind and (for goods out) types the vehicle number. Store reads the numbers from the photo later.

**Q: The network is down. Can the truck go?** A: Yes. Save the entry; the photos stay on the phone and upload when the network is back. Keep the phone on and on the network. The desk shows what is still to upload.

**Q: The visitor has no smartphone.** A: Use **+ Visitor** and fill the form for them, then **Check in and let in**.

**Q: Must the host approve before I let the visitor in?** A: No. You let them in after checking the ID; the host is told and can reply Send in, Ask to wait or Not available.

**Q: I picked Electricity but it was a material bill.** A: Ask Store or the plant head to use **Wrong type? Move it** in the Bills inbox. The bill then goes to Stores for the GRN.

**Q: The card says "Photo saved · Stores and Accounts open it". Why can't I see it?** A: The same file was already in the vault, or it is a kind your login may not open. The photo is safe.

**Q: Why is a visitor shown in red?** A: They are still inside after the gate close time. Mark them out when they leave; the Owner gets a daily list.

**Q: Who sees the visitors' phone numbers?** A: Only the watchman, the plant head, the Owner and the Admin. Others see them masked (98xxxxxx22).

**Q: The QR poster was posted on social media.** A: The Owner or Admin taps **New QR** in System › Gates and prints the new poster. The old QR stops working at once.

**Q: Can a watchman open the Doc Vault or other pages?** A: No. A watchman login opens only the gate desk, and sees only the photos they took today.


## From chapter 32: Doc Vault

**Q: Can I delete a photo I took by mistake?** A: No document is ever deleted. Ask the Owner or Admin to void it with a reason.

**Q: I took the photo but it does not show yet.** A: It is still on your phone waiting to upload. Keep the phone on the network; the strip at the top shows what is waiting.

**Q: Why can't I see the electricity bill?** A: Expense bills are seen by the plant head, Owner and Admin. Store sees material bills and challans; Dispatch sees outward papers.

**Q: The bill has three pages. Do I make three documents?** A: No. Take the first photo, then add the others with **Add page** at the gate, or **Photo of next page** in the viewer.

**Q: What is the Unlinked tab?** A: Vendor bills and challans not tied to a GRN yet. Link each one to its GRN.

**Q: Where are the debit note PDFs?** A: Each issued debit note's PDF is saved in the vault automatically and shows under **Documents** on the note.
