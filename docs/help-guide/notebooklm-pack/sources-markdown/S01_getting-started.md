# Getting started

> **What it is:** How to sign in, read the header bar, find pages in the sidebar, pick your plant and set light or dark mode.
> **Who uses it:** Every role.
> **Where:** Login page, then the header bar and sidebar on every page. Appearance help: Sidebar > Overview > Guide (route /help/appearance)

## At a glance
- Sign in with the **Username** and **Password** your Owner or Admin gave you. Sessions end after **15 minutes** without activity; signing in again takes you back to the same page.
- Each role lands on its own start page and sees only its own sidebar pages.
- The header bar holds search (**Jump to workspace**, Cmd+K or Ctrl+K), the season pill, theme and appearance, the month chip, the role switcher, the bell, the plant switcher and the page guide.
- Owner and Admin can view **All Visible Plants**, but that view is read-only. Any page that writes data asks you to pick one plant first.
- Theme (System, Light, Dark) and table density are saved on this device only. They never change your records or permissions.

Under the sign-in box a link, **New here? Read the help guide**, opens the handbook without signing in. After **Open ERP** you land on your role's start page. Check the plant name and the season pill, then open your work from the sidebar or the search. If the phone or PC is left idle for 15 minutes, you are signed out; when you sign in again the page you were on (for example a scanned job card) opens again.

3. **Jump to workspace** (⌘ K): search box that opens any page your role can use. See "How to find a page".
4. **Season pill**: **Rest of year** or **Monsoon**, the season used for new releases in both plants. It links to the Production season page. Only the Owner changes the season.
5. **Sun / moon button**: switches light and dark in one click.
6. **Appearance settings** (sliders icon): Theme **System / Light / Dark** and **Table density Comfortable / Compact**.
7. **Month chip**: shown to the Owner, Admin, Plant Manager, Planner and Store. **Close Sep** (amber) means September has ended and is still open; it opens Month-end close (Planner and Store go to the stock book). **Sep closed** (green) shows the last closed month; **Books open** means no month is closed yet. Entries dated in a closed month are refused.
8. **Role switcher**: shows your role. If your login has more than one role, pick another role here and you jump to that role's start page.
9. **Bell**: your inbox in a pop-up, with the unread count (red when something is urgent). See Dashboard and inbox.
10. **Plant switcher** (Owner and Admin only): **Plant A**, **Plant B** or **All Visible Plants**.
11. **Book icon** (Open page guide): opens the Guide for the page you are on.

Sidebar:

1. **Groups**: click a group name to open or close it. The group of the page you are on opens by itself, and the number beside a closed group is how many pages it holds for you.
2. **Sub-pages**: the open module shows its own pages underneath (for example Orders shows **Orders** and **Order close reasons**; Planner shows **Planning overview**, **Winder planning**, **Oven planning**, **Process planning**, **Slitting planning**).
3. **Badges**: **Inbox** unread count; **Orders** count of draft orders (amber); **Job Cards** overdue cards (red); **QC desk** open QC holds (amber).
4. **User box**: your name, your active role and the **Logout** button.

## Step by step

### How to sign in
1. Open the TubeOS address in Chrome (PC or phone).
2. Type your **Username** and **Password**. The password box has a show/hide eye.
3. Press **Open ERP**. While it works the button reads "Signing in…".
4. If the details are wrong, a red message shows under the password. Ask your Owner or Admin to reset it; TubeOS has no self-service password reset.
5. You land on your role's start page (table below). If you came from a scanned QR code or a link, that page opens instead.

### How to sign out
1. Click the **Logout** icon in the user box at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone: menu button › **Logout**).
2. You return to a plain login page. Always sign out on a shared floor tablet; floor-form drafts saved on the device belong to your login only.

### How to find a page (Jump to workspace)
1. Click **Jump to workspace** or press **Cmd+K** (Mac) / **Ctrl+K** (Windows) on any page.
2. With an empty box you see **Quick jump**: your first ten pages.
3. Type a word, for example "inward", "quality" or "calendar". Results show only pages your role may open.
4. Press **Enter** to open the first result, or **Arrow Down / Arrow Up** to move and **Enter** to open. **Esc** closes the box.
5. If nothing matches: "No matching workspace in your role." The search only opens pages; it never creates or changes records.

### How to switch role (logins with more than one role)
1. Open the role box in the header (on a phone, at the bottom of the menu panel).
2. Pick the role. You go to that role's start page and the Dashboard and Inbox queues follow that role.
3. Your sidebar does not shrink: it always shows the pages of every role your login holds. Switching role never changes who you are; for example, you still cannot approve an order you entered yourself (unless you are Owner or Admin).

### How to pick one plant (Owner and Admin)
1. Click the plant switcher on the right of the header.
2. Under **Select Plant**, choose **Plant A** or **Plant B**. **All Visible Plants** is marked "read-only scope".
3. The page reloads in that plant. Check the name in the switcher before entering anything.
4. Other roles have no switcher: your login decides your plant. If a scanned label belongs to another plant your login may open, TubeOS switches for you and says "Switched to Plant B for this label."

### Why a page says "select one plant"
Stock, purchases, receipts, planning and approvals belong to one plant. When the Owner or Admin is in **All Visible Plants**, those pages show a message such as "Choose one plant", "Pick one plant in the top switcher before approving", "Select one plant before scheduling" or, in the Owner's Action center, "Purchase queues need one plant — pick it from the plant selector." Pick the plant in the header and the page works normally. Reading lists across plants is fine; writing is not.

### How to set light, dark or System appearance and table density
1. For a quick change, click the **sun / moon** button. Light becomes dark, dark becomes light.
2. For the full choice, click **Appearance settings** (sliders icon). The panel says "Saved on this device only."
3. Under Theme choose **System** (follow the phone or PC setting, changing live when it changes), **Light** or **Dark**. New devices start on System.
4. Under **Table density** choose **Comfortable** (more space) or **Compact** (more rows on screen) on pages that support it.
5. Click outside the panel or press **Esc** to close it.
6. Job cards, spec print views and anything you print always stay white paper, even in dark mode.

### How to open help for the page you are on
1. Click the **book icon** at the far right of the header (hidden on very narrow phones).
2. The Guide opens on the guide for that page, with its working sequence, field rules and control checks.

## Rules the system enforces
- Session ends after 15 minutes without activity (automatic). The message reads "Your secure session ended after 15 minutes without activity. Sign in again to continue." If your access was changed, you see "Your session expired or your access was updated. Please sign in again."
- Pages your role cannot use are not in your sidebar or search. A direct link to such a page shows that it is not open to your role (BLOCKS).
- All Visible Plants is read-only; writing needs one plant (BLOCKS the write with a "pick one plant" message).
- Theme and density are stored in this browser only; another device keeps its own setting (automatic).
- Each role lands on its own page after sign-in or role switch (automatic).
- **Reasons are picked, not typed.** Wherever a screen asks why (a short close, a rejection, a hold, a cancel, a correction), you choose a code from a list and may add a note. Codes with OTHER need a note of at least 5 characters. Missing codes are added in Masters › Reason codes by a Plant Manager, Admin or the Owner (BLOCKS a save without a code).
- **Operators are picked from your department.** Floor forms list only the active employees of the stage's department in Masters › Employees (BLOCKS anyone else).

## Common questions
**Q: A form asks "Choose why". What do I pick?** A: The reason that fits best from the list. If none fits, pick the OTHER code and write a short note. Ask a Plant Manager, Admin or the Owner to add a new code if it comes up often.

**Q: I scanned a box label. What opens?** A: The box page: its pcs, where it is (packed, dispatched or kept as surplus), its QC flags and its job card. Phone cameras and the in-app **Scan** both work.

**Q: I cannot find a page my colleague uses.** A: It belongs to a role your login does not have. Ask the Owner or Admin to add the role in System › Users.

**Q: The plant switcher is missing.** A: Only the Owner and Admin can switch plants. Your plant comes from your login.

**Q: The page says "pick one plant" but I only have one plant.** A: You are probably in **All Visible Plants** (Owner/Admin). Choose Plant A or Plant B in the switcher.

**Q: Text looks wrong in dark mode.** A: Click the sun/moon button to go back to light, or set Appearance › **Light**. Tell the Admin which page looked wrong.

**Q: My phone keeps signing me out.** A: That is the 15-minute safety rule. Signing in again returns you to the same page.

**Q: What does the amber "Close Sep" chip mean?** A: September has ended but has not been closed. The Owner closes it in Month-end close after the count is approved.

**Q: I switched role but the sidebar did not change.** A: The sidebar always shows the pages of all your roles. The role switcher changes your start page and work queues.
