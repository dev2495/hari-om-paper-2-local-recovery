1

In one minute

Seven changes, each built from your decisions on 1 November.

Short close at any stage

What was made moves on to the next stage. The pieces not made go back to the sales order straight away, ready to release again or short close.

Boxes are the dispatch unit

Good Process pieces are packed into boxes as they are logged. Each box gets a code and a QR label and becomes finished stock that can ship at once.

QC only flags

A reading outside the band is flagged on the run, box and card. Job card QC never holds or rejects. Incoming paper QC still holds as before.

Reasons are codes

Every "why" in the system is picked from Masters › Reason codes, with a note when the code is Other. Typed-only reasons are refused.

Packing from masters

The spec picks box, plastic and fadda from the Packaging masters. A one-time Packing setup page fills all existing specs.

Your reel label

Reel and coil labels now copy your Excel label exactly, with a QR added and the AIT number in large letters.

Operators by department

Every floor entry asks for the operator and lists only that stage's department. A new Employee performance report shows each person's output.

Boxes, labels and the challan

Packing happens at the end of Process. The box is what ships.

Challan from boxesScan or tick boxes; partial dispatch any time
  • Boxes can ship while Process is still running, so partial output reaches the customer early. The card completes when every box has shipped.
  • The challan lists every box code, then "N boxes × pcs per box + part box", the box type, plastic and fadda per box from the spec.
  • Box labels print 8 or 21 to an A4 sheet, or one 100 × 75 mm sticker. Reprinting never adds stock. Scanning a box QR opens the box page.
  • Packing material use (boxes, plastic, fadda) is worked out per box from the master weights for the month-end comparison.

4

Job card QC flags, never holds

  • A reading outside its band is saved and flagged in amber on the run, the box, the card, the dispatch register and the challan.
  • No automatic hold is created and the challan is never blocked by a flag. Each check still needs 2 readings before a stage can close.
  • A plant head, Admin or Owner can still put a card on hold on purpose. Only such a hold stops dispatch.
  • Incoming paper QC is unchanged: an out-of-band reel is held until QC decides.
5

Every reason is a code

42 reason lists cover every action that asks "why", across sales, floor, QC, stores, purchase, month-end and setup.

  • Each screen shows a searchable list of the plant's active codes, plus a note. A code with OTHER in it needs a note of at least 5 characters.
  • The service that owns the record checks the code, so typed text alone is refused even through the API.
  • Records keep the code, the label at that moment and the note. Order and card histories, and the new Cancelled / short-closed by reason panel in Sales pulse, show the label.
  • Masters › Reason codes shows the lists in groups, with where each is used. Admins can rename, add or switch off codes.

6

Packing from the masters

  • The spec sheet picks the box, plastic and fadda from Masters › Packaging. With only one plastic and one fadda in the masters, they are filled in by themselves.
  • Sizes and per-piece weights come from the master, and month-end packing theory uses them.
  • A new spec cannot be approved without a box and pcs per box.
  • The Packing setup page (Design › Packing setup) lists every live spec. Set box and pcs per box row by row and save straight to the spec. Open job cards that have not packed a box yet pick up the change.

7

Reel and coil labels in your format

{{fig:reel-label|The new reel label: heading, mill, invoice no and date, plybond, variety and GSM, roll no, the AIT number in large letters, weight, and a QR.}}
  • New reels are numbered AIT 06283, AIT 06284 and so on, restarting at AIT 00001 every 1 April.
  • Because your own series is mid-year, the Owner sets the next number once in System › Plants (it can only go up, and needs a reason).
  • Coils slit from a reel carry the reel's number plus -1, -2. Old AT- numbers still scan and search.
  • The label heading comes from the plant (AMIGO INDUSTRIES UNIT II). The mill name comes from the vendor's short name.

Operators and the employee report

  • Log output and Log QC, on phone and PC, list only active employees of the department that runs the stage: winder, oven or process. The server refuses an operator from another department.
  • Reports › Employee performance shows runs, made, good, rejected, reject %, flagged QC readings, shifts and last activity per operator, with drill-down and CSV.

Before switching on

These need your people on the live system. Each takes minutes, except packing setup.

  1. Set every operator's departmentMasters › Employees. Without it the floor forms offer no operator for that stage.
  2. Add the missing box mastersBox codes used on specs but missing from Masters › Packaging (for example R.125, R.150, N-225, S-300, EXPORT). Packing setup lists them with an Add box link.
  3. Fill packing on every live specHarish uses Design › Packing setup once. New specs need it before approval.
  4. Set the next AIT numberSystem › Plants: enter the number after the last reel label printed from Excel.
  5. Set mill short names on vendorsMasters › Vendors, so labels print "AKSHAT" rather than the full company name.
  6. Review the reason listsMasters › Reason codes. Rename or add codes in your own words, and switch off any you will not use.
  7. Clear test data on the live systemTest reasons, test orders and test cards from earlier trials, with a reviewed script.
10

Please confirm three choices

Part box bigger than the room left

Example: 1,000 released and 900 shipped, but the last part box holds 150. The customer gets 900, the 100 pcs of room go back to the order, and the 150-pc box stays as surplus stock.

Two part boxes after a reopen

If Process is reopened after its part box was made, the old part box stays and the new runs make another. Boxes are physical, so they are not merged.

Paper theory at Process close

Boxes post their packing use as they are packed. Paper use for the card posts when Process closes, as before. A month closed mid-Process shows packing but not paper for that card.

Fadda is treated as the putha used inside the box.
